Testing VoiceOver groups

VoiceOver may treat some parts of a page as groups. A group is a container that VoiceOver moves past as one item until you choose to go inside it. Whether something is treated as a group can depend on your browser and VoiceOver settings.

Move through each example and listen for whether VoiceOver treats it as one item. If it does, try entering and leaving it.

  • To enter a group: press VO + Shift + Down Arrow (interact).
  • To move through items inside a group: press VO + Right Arrow or VO + Left Arrow.
  • To leave a group: press VO + Shift + Up Arrow (stop interacting).

Example 4: Toolbar

The toolbar is a single Tab stop. Once focus is in the toolbar, use Left Arrow and Right Arrow to move between buttons, and Home and End to jump to the first and last button.

HTML markup 
<div role="toolbar" aria-label="Text formatting">
  <button type="button" tabindex="0">Bold</button>
  <button type="button" tabindex="-1">Italic</button>
  <button type="button" tabindex="-1">Underline</button>
</div>

Roving tabindex: only one button in the toolbar has tabindex="0" at a time, so Tab enters and leaves the toolbar as one stop. When an arrow key moves focus, the newly focused button gets tabindex="0" and the others get tabindex="-1". If you Tab away and back, focus returns to the last button you used.
