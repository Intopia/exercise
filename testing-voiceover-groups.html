Testing VoiceOver groups

VoiceOver may treat some parts of a page as groups. A group is a container that VoiceOver moves past as one item until you choose to go inside it. Whether something is treated as a group can depend on your browser and VoiceOver settings.

Move through each example and listen for whether VoiceOver treats it as one item. If it does, try entering and leaving it.

  • To enter a group: press VO + Shift + Down Arrow (interact).
  • To move through items inside a group: press VO + Right Arrow or VO + Left Arrow.
  • To leave a group: press VO + Shift + Up Arrow (stop interacting).
