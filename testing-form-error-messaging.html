Testing form field error messaging

Error messages should be informative and contextual. Where possible, messages should:

  • Clearly explain what went wrong and how to fix it.
  • Relate directly to the specific field.

Example 1: Simple message

Please provide your name.

Example 2: Simple message

This field is required.

Example 3: Contextual message

Phone number must include area code.
